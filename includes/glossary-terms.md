*[AFS]: Available for sale: securities carried at fair value; their unrealised gains and losses go to equity (AOCI), not profit.
*[HTM]: Held to maturity: securities carried at amortised cost; their unrealised gains and losses are not booked.
*[AOCI]: Accumulated other comprehensive income: unrealised gains and losses on AFS securities, held in equity.
*[OCI]: Other comprehensive income: gains and losses recorded in equity rather than profit.
*[CET1]: Common Equity Tier 1: the highest-quality regulatory capital, mainly common equity less intangibles.
*[AT1]: Additional Tier 1: capital such as preferred stock that counts in Tier 1 but not in CET1.
*[RWA]: Risk-weighted assets: exposures weighted by their riskiness; capital ratios divide by them.
*[EVE]: Economic value of equity: the present value of the assets less that of the liabilities.
*[NII]: Net interest income: interest earned less interest paid.
*[NIM]: Net interest margin: net interest income as a share of interest-earning assets.
*[IRRBB]: Interest rate risk in the banking book.
*[FVOCI]: Fair value through other comprehensive income: the IFRS 9 counterpart of AFS.
*[FVTPL]: Fair value through profit or loss: marked to market, with gains and losses in profit.
*[CMT]: Constant maturity Treasury yields, published daily by the US Treasury.
*[bp]: Basis point: one hundredth of a percentage point (100bp = 1%).
*[ROA]: Return on assets: profit after tax over average total assets.
*[ROE]: Return on equity: profit after tax over average equity.
*[LTV]: Loan-to-value ratio: a mortgage's balance over the value of the property.
*[MBS]: Mortgage-backed security: a bond paid from a pool of mortgages, which can be prepaid early.
*[LCR]: Liquidity Coverage Ratio: liquid assets over 30 days of stressed outflows.
*[NSFR]: Net Stable Funding Ratio: stable funding over the funding the assets need.
*[FDIC]: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, which publishes US banks' Call Reports.
